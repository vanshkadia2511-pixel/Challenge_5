# KSHAN — Demo Walkthrough Script (2:30 Video)

Step-by-step video presentation guide for PromptWars Virtual (Challenge 5: Celebration & Event Planner).

---

## Act 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:30)
- **Visual:** Landing Page at `http://localhost:5173`.
- **Narration:**
  > "Most celebration planning apps ask you to manage vendors: caterers, florists, DJs, venues. But nobody remembers a celebration for the line items on an invoice — they remember the moments. When couples or families plan, they run out of money on things guests barely notice, while the signature moments get squeezed.
  >
  > Welcome to **KSHAN** — the event planner where you **budget the moments, not the vendors**."

---

## Act 2: Moment Deck & Story Interview (0:30 – 1:00)
- **Visual:** Click "Wedding" template $\rightarrow$ Moment Deck Page.
- **Action:** Show the 8 signature wedding moments (Welcome, Family Blessing, Ceremony, Portraits, Toasts, Dinner, First Dance, Send-off).
- **Action:** Click "Story Interview" button. Enter answers: 150 guests, Mumbai, ₹5 Lakhs, Intimate vibe.
- **Visual:** Review Card appears with "Suggested by Gemini" badge. Click **Apply to Deck**.
- **Narration:**
  > "Instead of staring at a blank spreadsheet, you pick and rank the beats of your day. You can tune energy targets, lock must-have moments, or let our Story Interview transcribe your narrative into moments. Notice the Review Card: AI proposes ideas, but you decide whether to Apply, Edit, or Dismiss."

---

## Act 3: Plan Workspace & Trade-off Engine (1:00 – 1:40)
- **Visual:** Click "Plan Workspace".
- **Action:** Show the Moment Budget table. Direct and shared costs are attributed to each moment. Point out the protected 10% contingency.
- **Action:** Click **Trade-off** on "Couple Portraits".
- **Visual:** The Trade-off Drawer slides open, calculating freed funds, redistributed money across other moments, removed tasks, and vendor impacts.
- **Action:** Click **Apply Trade-off**.
- **Narration:**
  > "In the Plan Workspace, our pure math engine calculates the exact cost per moment using the largest-remainder method. When money gets tight, test a trade-off. Cut a moment and see exactly how much cash is freed and how other moments are upgraded before you commit. No guesswork, no math drift."

---

## Act 4: Energy Curve & Vendor Cue Sheets (1:40 – 2:05)
- **Visual:** Click "Energy Curve".
- **Action:** Hover over the SVG arc. Show the transition flags and accessible table equivalent below.
- **Visual:** Click "Moment Book" $\rightarrow$ scroll to Vendor Cue Sheets.
- **Narration:**
  > "Great celebrations have an intentional emotional rhythm. The Energy Curve analyzes your timeline, flagging awkward lulls or rushed transitions. And on the big day, you don't hand vendors a giant binder. KSHAN generates clean, print-ready Cue Sheets for each vendor with their exact times, moments, and specific cues."

---

## Act 5: Moment Book & After-Event Log (2:05 – 2:30)
- **Visual:** Moment Book print preview (`window.print()` trigger).
- **Visual:** Click "After-Event" tab.
- **Action:** Show the Reality Scorecard: Planned vs Actual spend, net variance, and Keep/Skip ratings.
- **Narration:**
  > "Finally, print your complete Moment Book or export to CSV. And after the event, log what actually happened: track your variance and rate moments keep or skip so you know what truly mattered.
  >
  > KSHAN: Budget the moments, not the vendors."
